A.J. Diebold proposed an inter-club competition in the summer of 1945. The
organizing meeting was held at the Duquesne Club that August.

The first captains were Joe Oliver (Fox Chapel), John McGraw (Field Club) and
Andy Stover (Oakmont), with Stover serving as secretary.

The original bylaws specify:

- eight-man teams
- four-ball groupings scored as individual matches
- Nassau scoring
- no stymies
- amateur members only
- the winning club keeps the trophy until the next competition

Fox Chapel took the first cup in September 1945, over three matches among the
three founding clubs. Allegheny Country Club joined in 1946 as the fourth seat.
